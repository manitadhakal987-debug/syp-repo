Managing Curls in Nepal's Changing Seasons

Monsoon & Summer Humidity

Excess atmospheric moisture causes curl strands to swell and frizz. Use strong-hold botanical flaxseed gels and seal the cast with a lightweight oil (Argan or Jojoba).

Winter Dryness & Dust

Cold, dry Kathmandu valley air siphons moisture from cuticles. Switch to deep conditioning masks weekly and sleep in a satin bonnet to prevent friction breakage.
